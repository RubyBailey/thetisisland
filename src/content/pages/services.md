## Trails

TIRRA manages a network of volunteer-maintained walking trails which provide safe access to natural areas. Please respect private property boundaries and tread lightly to protect sensitive habitats.

We extend our sincere appreciation to the many landowners who generously allow public access across their properties. Their ongoing support makes these trails possible and provides an invaluable recreational resource for the entire community. We are grateful for their trust and continued partnership in preserving one of the unique qualities that make Thetis Island such a special place to live and visit.

If you’d like to help maintain the trails or join a trail work party, please use our [contact form](/contact). Someone from the trails committee will get back to you.

## Communications

Community communications and information resources help residents and visitors stay informed.

### e-Spokes Email List

e-Spokes is the island's free, moderated email bulletin board for community notices, announcements, lost and found items, and service information. Subscribers' email addresses are not shared publicly.

To subscribe, email [espokes@gmail.com](mailto:espokes@gmail.com) with your name and a request to join, or use the [online subscription form](https://subscribepage.io/KUKwRy). Once approved, you will begin receiving messages. Submitted posts should be concise and relevant to the community.

### Thetis Island Phone Book

Compiled by community volunteers and distributed as a printed booklet, the Thetis Island Phone Book includes residents' landline and cell numbers, emergency contacts and other useful information. It is not available online for privacy reasons.

If you are new to Thetis or need a copy, get in touch through our [contact page](/contact).

### Welcome Package

New residents can receive a Welcome Package containing a Thetis Island Phone Book and essential information about local contacts, services and community guidelines. Request a copy through our [contact page](/contact).

### Visitors' Guide

The Thetis Island Visitors' Guide includes an island map and information about local accommodations, artists, shopping, food, dining and catering, along with other general island information.

[Download the current Thetis Island Visitors' Guide](/visitor-guide).

## Solid Waste Management

The solid waste yard accepts regular household garbage on a pay-per-bag basis. Construction and renovation debris are not accepted, and there is no recycling drop-off facility on Thetis Island. Please reduce waste, reuse and recycle wherever possible; short-term visitors may find it more convenient to take their garbage with them when leaving.

**Hours:** Typically Wednesday and Saturday, 9:00 am–12:00 pm. Check the hours posted at the gate and e-Spokes for changes.

**Location:** 501 Pilkey Point Road, about a kilometer from the ferry landing. Please drive slowly and follow the attendants' directions.

**Returnables:** Rinsed and sorted returnable beverage containers can be dropped off at any time in the collection bins beside the solid waste facility. Refund proceeds support the Thetis Island Nature Conservancy (ThINC) and its nature education, conservation and stewardship programs.

## Roads & Transportation

While road maintenance on Thetis is under local and provincial jurisdiction, we serve as a liaison between residents, the Cowichan Valley Regional District (CVRD) and the Ministry of Transportation. If you notice road issues (potholes, drainage problems, signage), report them through our [contact page](/contact) so we can advocate for repairs.

## St. Margaret's Cemetery

"To preserve the Cemetery as a place of quiet tranquility in a park like setting. To carry on its affairs responsibly and with sensitivity for the bereaved and for the memory of the people buried there."

St. Margaret's Cemetery is a registered charity maintained and administered by volunteers on behalf of the community. Plots are available for Thetis Island residents and property owners. The cemetery committee oversees landscaping and coordinates burials.

For information about plot availability, fees or regulations, please contact the cemetery coordinator at cemetery@thetisisland.net. Donations to support ongoing maintenance are appreciated.
