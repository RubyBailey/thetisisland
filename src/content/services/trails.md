Thetis Island has an expanding network of trails maintained by volunteers. Trails provide safe walking routes and access to natural areas. Please respect private property boundaries and tread lightly to protect sensitive habitats.

TIRRA extends its sincere appreciation to the many landowners who generously allow public access across their properties. Their ongoing support makes these trails possible and provides an invaluable recreational resource for the entire community. We are grateful for the trust they place in TIRRA and for their continued partnership in preserving one of the unique qualities that make Thetis Island such a special place to live and visit.

If you are interested in helping with trail maintenance or joining a trail work party, get in touch with the trails committee.
